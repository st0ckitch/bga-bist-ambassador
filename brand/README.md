# Circle — brand marks

The mark is two arcs with pill-rounded caps, rotationally offset so they close
into one ring with two deliberate gaps. Two schools completing one circle; the
gaps keep it open, because the partner network is still growing.

Open `brand/index.html` in a browser to see every cut at the sizes it actually
ships at, in place in the nav, the band and the footer.

## Files

| File | Use |
| --- | --- |
| `circle-mark.svg` | Standard cut. Above 32px, on light backgrounds. |
| `circle-mark-small.svg` | Small cut. **32px and below on light backgrounds** — including the nav. |
| `circle-mark-reverse.svg` | On navy `#0D1B3E`: cream + gold. |
| `circle-mark-navy.svg` / `circle-mark-gold.svg` | One-colour, for embroidery, stamps, partner windows. |
| `circle-favicon.svg` | Browser tab. Navy tile, cropped tighter than the standard cut. |
| `circle-chip.svg` | 58×38 card chip, for the gold slot in the navy band. |
| `circle-lockup.svg` | Reference artwork for mark + wordmark. |
| `circle-segmented.svg` / `circle-monogram.svg` | Alternates, kept for comparison. Not in use. |

Everything is generated by `build-marks.py` from one construction circle
(centre 50,50 in a 100×100 viewBox, radius 36), so the family stays
geometrically consistent. Edit the script, not the SVGs:

```sh
python3 brand/build-marks.py
```

## Two things that are not arbitrary

**The small cut exists because the standard one fails at nav size.** Gold
`#F2B32C` on cream `#F6F4EE` is low enough in contrast that at 22px the lower
arc drops away and the ring reads lopsided rather than circular. The small cut
takes the stroke from 12 to 14 and the gold to `#D99A14` — the deeper stop of
the card gradient already used on the site. Verified by rasterising at 1× and
magnifying, not by eyeballing a large render.

**The favicon carries a navy tile and a stroke of 16.** Untiled, the gold arc
disappears against a light tab bar. Thinner than 16 and the two arcs mush into
a blob at 16px. Wider gaps than the 40° used here stop reading as one closed
ring at that size.

## Lockup

Mark, then `CIRCLE` in Sora 800, all caps, `.22em` tracking — the treatment
already used on the site. The `BGA • BIST` pill stays a separate endorsement
element and is not part of the logo.

Clear space on all sides is one stroke width of the cut in use.
